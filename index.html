An AI startup · Data ontology

Hard problems can be solved when people understand their data. Thothica helps them do that by building ontologies and AI-native software.

Thothica is an AI startup. We build ontologies, and we build software on top of them.

An ontology is a precise written record of what an organization's data means. It states what each item is, how the items relate to one another, and who is responsible for each decision.

When software runs on an ontology, your staff and your AI systems work from the same definitions, and your organization no longer depends on a few experienced people to interpret its data.

You can then solve, at scale, the problems those people used to solve one at a time.

What we build

  1. 1

    We build approval systems for governments. The software checks each application against every requirement, shows the applicant what is missing, and shows the official what is ready for a decision. Approvals move faster because everyone can see what is pending.

  2. 2

    For one state government in India, we built the AI layer that now runs on five live portals. The layer reads each portal's own records and answers officials' questions from them, and it cannot change the underlying system.

  3. 3

    We integrate AI layers into legacy software. Your existing system stays as it is. The layer reads the system's data, explains it in plain language, and answers questions that staff would otherwise answer by searching through many screens and files.

  4. 4

    For a think tank, we rebuilt an archive of more than 1,000 historical works that ran on a slow, outdated website. It is now a fast digital library. Researchers can search it in five languages, and every answer points back to the original document.

  5. 5

    We run Falsafa.ai, our open-source library of the world's philosophical, classical and religious texts. It holds more than 2,000 works, split into more than 1.3 million passages. People and AI systems can search the library and quote it with exact citations.

  6. 6

    We produce research-based graphic stories at scale. Our software keeps the characters and the drawing style consistent from page to page, and it checks every statement in the story against a source.
